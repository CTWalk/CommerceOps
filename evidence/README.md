# Verified CommerceOps evidence

This directory contains only first-party CommerceOps product evidence selected for the public QA/SDET showcase.

The primary scene set was curated from the accepted CommerceOps product baseline `817b4f136e0d24099a0ee4661d75f3e69e9b9fb6`. The three responsive Reports captures come from its immediate predecessor; the intervening change was a Maestro assertion-only change, so their rendered product UI remained representative of the accepted baseline.

The donor archive recorded every retained scene as visually inspected, readable, useful beyond diagnostics, non-duplicate, free of secrets/private credentials/machine-path overlays, and free of third-party reference UI.

## Groups

- `product/` — customer journey, important product states, and staff operational surfaces.
- `refund-lifecycle/` — the cross-role refund narrative.
- `rendered-ui/` — accepted Reports views plus the deterministic canvas regression baseline.
- `mobile/` — packaged Android entry/lifecycle context.

These images are evidence of the accepted product scenes they document; they are not a claim that every later private commit changed no UI.
